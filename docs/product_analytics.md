# Product Analytics — Sample Questions

These are the types of product-focused questions that commercial analytics users ask. The assistant should handle these across different time periods, geographies, and user roles.

## Product Performance & Ranking

- "How is Zenovax performing this quarter in pack units?"
- "What are total pack units sold for Carbotrel in the last 6 months?"
- "Show me monthly Gemtara volume for the past year"
- "Which NovaPharma product has the highest volume?"
- "Rank all our branded products by total pack units this year"

## Product Comparison

- "Compare Zenovax vs Carbotrel volume over the last 12 months"
- "How does Luprex Depot volume compare to our oncology products?"
- "Which NovaPharma product grew the fastest quarter over quarter?"
- "Show me all NovaPharma products side by side for the current quarter"

## Competitive Landscape

- "How does Zenovax volume compare to Taxotere and Docetaxel Generic?"
- "What is the generic share in the Platinum Compounds market?"
- "Which competitor drugs are gaining volume in Antimetabolites?"
- "Show me all products in the Taxanes market category and their volume"
- "How is Pemetrexed Generic trending vs our Paxelium?"

## Market Share

- "What is our market share for Zenovax in the Docetaxel market?"
- "How has Carbotrel market share changed over the last 4 quarters?"
- "Which territories have below-average market share for Gemtara?"
- "Compare our market share across all market categories"
- "Where are we losing share in the Platinum Compounds market?"
- "Show me Zenovax market share by territory"

## Dosing & Formulation

- "What is the volume split between Zenovax 80MG/4ML and 20MG/1ML?"
- "Which strength of Carbotrel (450MG vs 150MG) has higher volume?"
- "Show me the NDC-level volume breakdown for Cyclonova"
- "How does injectable vs oral volume split in the urology market?"

## Data Source & Channel

- "What is Zenovax distributor volume vs total market data volume?"
- "How much hub dispense (free drug) volume do we have for Cyclonova?"
- "Show me the data source breakdown for all NovaPharma products"
- "What percentage of Gemtara total volume comes through hub dispense?"

## Market Category Analysis

- "How is our oncology portfolio performing overall in pack units?"
- "Compare total NovaPharma urology volume to competitor volume"
- "What is the total market size for Alkylating Agents?"
- "Show me the Antiemetics market — NovaPharma vs competitors"
- "Which market category has the most competitor products?"
- "What is total volume across the GnRH Agonists market?"

## Product by Geography

- "Which region has the highest Zenovax volume?"
- "Show me Luprex Depot volume by territory"
- "Compare Paxelium pack units across the Northeast vs Southeast"
- "What are the top 5 states for Oncosetron volume?"

## Trend & Growth

- "Is Zenovax volume growing or declining month over month?"
- "Show me the year-over-year growth rate for each NovaPharma product"
- "Which products are trending up in pack units over the last 3 months?"
- "What is the rolling 3-month average volume for Paxelium?"
- "Compare Q1 2026 vs Q1 2025 for all oncology products"
