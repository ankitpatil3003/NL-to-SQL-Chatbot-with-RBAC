# Account Analytics — Sample Questions

These are the types of account-focused questions that commercial analytics users ask day-to-day. A well-built assistant should handle these naturally.

## Account Performance & Ranking

- "What are my top 10 accounts by pack units this quarter?"
- "Which accounts have declined more than 20% in volume vs prior quarter?"
- "Rank all accounts in the Northeast region by total pack units"
- "Which accounts had the most growth year over year?"
- "Show me the bottom 5 accounts by volume this month"

## Account Trending

- "Show me the monthly volume trend for my largest account over the last 6 months"
- "What is the quarter-over-quarter volume trend for my top 3 health systems?"
- "Which accounts are trending down in distributor volume over the last 3 months?"

## Organization Hierarchy

- "Roll up all facilities under a specific health system and show total volume"
- "Which health systems (grandparent orgs) have the most facilities?"
- "Show me the parent-level breakdown for a given health system"
- "How many active facilities does each health system have?"
- "List all standalone facilities (no parent hierarchy) and their total volume"

## Territory & Region

- "Give me an account summary for all organizations in the Northeast region"
- "How are territories in the Midwest performing this quarter vs last quarter?"
- "Rank all territories by total NovaPharma volume"
- "Which territory has the fastest growth in pack units?"
- "Compare account counts across all territories"

## GPO Affiliation

- "What is our total volume by GPO — Onmark, ION, Unity, VitalSource?"
- "Compare Onmark vs ION affiliated accounts by total pack units"
- "Which GPO has the most member health systems?"
- "Show me all accounts affiliated with VitalSource and their volume"

## Archetype & Specialty

- "Compare hospital vs clinic accounts by total volume"
- "Which hospital accounts have the highest NovaPharma volume?"
- "How many specialty pharmacy accounts do we sell to?"
- "Show me all government accounts and their volume"
- "What is the oncology vs urology split across my accounts?"

## 340B Program

- "Show me all 340B accounts and their volume"
- "How does 340B volume compare to non-340B volume?"
- "Which 340B accounts have the highest pack units?"
- "What percentage of total volume comes from 340B accounts?"

## Account by Data Source

- "What is the distributor vs market data volume split across my top accounts?"
- "Which accounts have the most hub dispense (free drug) volume?"
- "Show me accounts where hub dispense is more than 10% of total volume"

## Multi-Turn Conversations

Users often follow up on previous questions:

1. "What are my top 5 accounts?" → results shown
2. "Now break that down by quarter" → refines the previous query
3. "How does that compare to last year?" → adds time comparison
4. "Exclude 340B accounts" → further filters

The assistant should maintain conversation context and refine queries based on follow-ups.
